I compared multiple machine learning models to predict which telecom customers were most likely to leave. Logistic Regression became the final selected model because it delivered the best held-out business value, AUC-ROC, and AUC-PR when using a cost-weighted threshold to maximize retention ROI. I built a dashboard that turns churn scores into a prioritized retention list showing who is most at risk, how much revenue is exposed, which customers should be contacted first, and includes an ROI calculator to estimate campaign value before committing budget.
